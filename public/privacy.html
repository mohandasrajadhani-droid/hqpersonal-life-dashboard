Privacy Policy — Personal Life Dashboard

This is a self-hosted, personal-use application. This instance is operated by its deployer for their own use (and anyone they've invited to register an account here) — not a commercial or public service.

What data is collected

Only what you enter directly into the app: tasks, reminders, bills, expenses, income, medicines, appointments, medical records, blood pressure readings, renewals, calendar events, trips, habits, credit card metadata (never full card numbers, CVV, or PINs — the app actively rejects those), bank account metadata, and your account email/name/password.

How it's stored

All personal, financial, and health records are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM before being written to disk. Passwords are hashed with scrypt and never stored in plain text. If this instance is connected to a Google Drive backend, the same encrypted files (not the underlying plaintext) are also backed up to a Google Drive folder — see this project's GOOGLE_DRIVE_SETUP.md for exactly what that involves.

What it's used for

Solely to run the dashboard for the account that entered it. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used to train any model.

Third parties

No data is sent to third parties, with the narrow exception of the optional Google Drive backend described above (which only this app's own encrypted backup files pass through, using the drive.file scope — meaning it can only see files it created itself, never anything else in that Drive account).

Your data, your control

The app includes an in-app account deletion feature that permanently purges all of your records. Because this is self-hosted software, the person operating this specific instance is its data controller — contact them directly (see the app's sign-in screen or repository) with any questions or requests.
